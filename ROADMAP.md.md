## 📌 HOW TO USE THIS FILE

1. This plan is spread over **12 months (48 weeks)** instead of 10 — the extra ~8 weeks are your **built-in buffer**, so missing 1–2 days every 2–3 weeks will NOT break your schedule.
2. Track progress by ticking `[ ]` → `[x]` for each item. Re-save the file every week (or copy it into Notion/Google Docs/OneNote if you want tick-boxes on your phone).
3. **Weekly target = 18 hours**, not "3 hours x 6 days no matter what." If one day is missed, make it up across the remaining days that week. Only if a full week is lost, use a **Buffer Week** (marked below) to catch up.
4. Every month has **1 built-in Buffer/Revision Week** already counted in — so the 4 "content weeks" shown per month are really spread across ~4.5–5 real weeks.
5. Rule of thumb: **Don't move to the next topic until the checkboxes of the current one are honestly tickable.**

## 🧠 Buffer Philosophy (read this when you feel behind)

- Missing 1–2 days in 3 weeks is **already accounted for** — that's exactly why this is a 12-month plan instead of 10.
- Never try to "double up" hours to compensate for a missed day — just extend that week's topic by a day and shift everything else by one day.
- If you fall a full week behind, use your **monthly Buffer/Revision Week** (the implicit 5th week each month) to catch up — do NOT skip topics to stay "on schedule." Understanding > calendar.
